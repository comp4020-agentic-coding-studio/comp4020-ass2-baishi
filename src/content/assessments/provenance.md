## The brief

> Make an object, and make the paper trail that would convince a sceptical
> buyer it's real.

Provenance is the semester's argument, made in one piece of work: that an
object's claimed history can matter as much as the object itself, and that
both halves are craft, and both are fakeable, and both get checked
differently. Choose your subject as early as week 8 — the studios that week
onward are built around having something concrete to develop. The material
can be anything the semester has touched: a painting with an invented
exhibition history, a manuscript with a forged correspondence trail, a
currency design with a fabricated minting record, a codebase with a rewritten
commit history and a laundered licence.

## What you submit

The object itself, in whatever form suits the material, and a provenance
file: the documents, records or history that would make a sceptical buyer or
reviewer believe it's genuine. Both halves are presented and defended in
person at the week 12 final crit, where the room questions the provenance
file the way real due diligence would.
