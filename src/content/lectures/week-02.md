Connoisseurship gets talked about as if it were a sixth sense — an expert
walks into a room, looks at a canvas for four seconds, and knows. It isn't
a sense; it's a checklist run so fast it feels like one: brushwork rhythm,
underdrawing, the chemistry of the pigment against the claimed date, the wear
pattern on the frame against the wear pattern on the story. A forger has to
get every item on that list right simultaneously, forwards, while the
connoisseur only has to find the one item that's wrong. That asymmetry is why
forgery is hard and detection, comparatively, is not — and it's the shape of
every detection problem the rest of the semester touches, paint or otherwise.
