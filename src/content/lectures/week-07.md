A painting with a documented, unbroken chain of ownership back to the artist's
studio is worth more than an identical painting with a gap in that chain — not
because the paper trail changes the paint, but because provenance is doing the
authentication work the object itself often can't. Which means provenance is
just as fakeable as the object, and forging a chain of exhibition catalogues,
sale receipts and letters is a distinct craft from forging a canvas, with its
own literature of scandal. This lecture is the pivot from making a convincing
object to making a convincing history for one — the through-line into the
final project.
