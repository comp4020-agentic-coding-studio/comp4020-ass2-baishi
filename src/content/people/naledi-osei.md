Naledi builds the detection tooling that broadcasters and archives use to flag
manipulated video and audio before it airs, which means her working week is mostly
spent looking at fakes good enough that a person couldn't have caught them. She
guests for the two weeks where the course's materials stop being physical: the same
questions about technique, tell and provenance turn out to still apply, just moved
into pixels.

She's blunt about the limits of her own field in the room, which is exactly why
Petra invited her: a course about fakes that never admits what its own experts
can't yet catch would be telling one.
