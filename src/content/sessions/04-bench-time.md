## Before the session

Bring your Assignment 1 work in progress and the style or object you're
copying, so a tutor can look at both side by side.

## In the session

No lecture and no new demonstration this week — this studio is deliberately
open bench time. Anselm circulates and answers questions one bench at a time
rather than teaching to the room, which is closer to how a real studio
practice actually runs than a scheduled demo every week would be.

## Afterwards

There's no formal check-in beyond conversation at the bench, but showing up
with nothing since week 3 is the one way to make this week useless to
yourself.
