# A copy declares itself. A forgery doesn't.

That's the whole difference. Not skill, not intent — one missing piece of
paper.

## Four materials, one semester

- paint
- prose
- currency
- code

Same argument, different chemistry.

---

## Why fake it to understand it

Failing to convincingly copy something is the fastest way to find out what
you left out of your understanding of the real thing.

A forger learns this earlier than anyone else does.

---

## The connoisseur's asymmetry

A forger has to get **every** detail right, at once, forwards.

A detector only has to find the **one** detail that's wrong.

That asymmetry is the shape of every detection problem this semester touches.

## What this course is not

Not a course in getting away with it.

Every graded piece carries a materials-and-method note — the declaration
that turns the semester's own fakes back into copies.
